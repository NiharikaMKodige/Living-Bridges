Street dog care runs on goodwill. It needs a shared memory.

Living Bridges connects the people who look after street dogs: feeders, neighbours, NGOs and municipal teams. We are starting with the piece everything else depends on, knowing exactly which dog is which.

We begin with identification: recognise a dog from a short nose video and give it a permanent ID, all inside WhatsApp.

  • Collars don’t last

    Many street dogs slip or chew them off, so a tag alone leaves gaps.

  • Care changes hands

    Morning and night feeders, vets and drive teams all need the same record.

  • Records get repeated

    Without a reliable ID, dogs are vaccinated or sterilised twice, or missed entirely.

From a short video to a known dog

Everything happens inside the WhatsApp thread caregivers already use. The nose-print matching builds on published research in canine biometrics, including the paper “AI-Driven Canine Dog Nose Print Recognition”.

Record

Film the dog’s nose for two seconds from a safe distance, even while it moves.

Clean up

The system picks the sharpest frame, crops the snout and evens out glare and dirt.

Match

The nose ridge pattern becomes a digital signature, checked against every registered dog.

Reply

You get the dog’s ID, area, vaccination and sterilisation status in the same chat.

Questions, answered

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everything happens in a WhatsApp chat you already have. There is no separate app and no login.

How does a nose scan work?

You record a two-second video of the dog’s nose. We pick the sharpest frame, read the ridge pattern, and match it against registered dogs. The reply arrives in the same chat.

What if the dog won’t stay still or its nose is muddy?

Just try again. The bot tells you if the image was too blurry or dark and asks for another quick clip.

Is my phone number shared with others?

No. Caregiver contact details are kept behind anonymous tokens, so private numbers are never shown publicly.

Can a dog’s nose print change over time?

Nose texture can shift slightly with age, injury or scarring. If a match is uncertain, the bot asks you to try again rather than guess.
